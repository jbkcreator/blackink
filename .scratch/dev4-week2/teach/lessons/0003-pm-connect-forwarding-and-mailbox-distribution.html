How a PM connects — inbound & mailbox distribution

The onboarding handshake, the forwarded-email path, and who gets which mailboxes. Grounded in Blueprint v2.

The problem this solves: a property manager already gets owner inquiries — from their website, from Zillow/Trulia, from portal notification emails. Blackink has to catch every one of those without asking the PM to rebuild their world. So there are two connection styles: a clean webhook when the source can push, and a forwarding address when it can't.

Part 1 — The onboarding handshake (how the PM connects)

During onboarding, two states wire the PM in. Blueprint v2 States 12 & 13:

State 12 — Sending Identity & Compliance • assign the client's 3 dedicated sending domains • configure a delegated subdomain (e.g. {client}.getblackink.com) • set Reply-To = the PM's OWN address ← replies land in their real inbox • BCC the PM on every send ← full visibility • build bidirectional non-poach suppression lists State 13 — Campaign & Lead Routing • register inbound lead-capture webhook (Path A) • provision parse address leads@{client}.getblackink.com (Path B)

So "connecting" = the PM points their lead sources at Blackink, and Blackink sends as the PM (Reply-To + BCC) so it looks and feels like the PM's own mail.

Source: Blueprint v2, onboarding States 12–13 (§ ~lines 775–776).

Part 2 — The forwarded-email path (Path B)

This is the key one you asked about. Many lead sources (Zillow, Trulia, HotPads, MLS forms) only send a notification email — they can't call a webhook. So the PM sets up a forwarding rule: any inquiry notification is auto-forwarded to their Blackink parse address.

Owner submits inquiry on Zillow │ ▼ Zillow emails the PM: "New inquiry for 123 Main St…" │ │ PM's mailbox has a FORWARD RULE: │ from Zillow ▶ forward to leads@{client}.getblackink.com ▼ leads@{client}.getblackink.com (Mailgun Route, HMAC-signed) │ Inbound Parse Hook (<5s) ▼ parse the forwarded body by source: native form? Zillow? APM? Thumbtack? → regex extract (name, phone, property address, inquiry text) │ ▼ Speed-to-Lead orchestrator → non-poach gate → inbound_messages ▼ 30-min auto-response + booking link + closer card ▶ #blackink-setter
Why the subdomain matters: the address is leads@{client-subdomain}.getblackink.com. The subdomain names the tenant — that's how the parser knows which client_id a forwarded email belongs to, without the PM doing anything special. One Mailgun catch-all route serves every client (see Lesson 2).

Source: Blueprint v2, Path B "Email-Parsing Fallback" (§ ~line 684) + dual-ingestion flow (§ ~663–681).

Part 3 — Mailbox distribution: 20 domains / 40 mailboxes

The pool is split into internal (Blackink's own marketing) and client-dedicated pools. Every domain carries exactly 2 mailboxes.

TOTAL: 20 domains × 2 mailboxes = 40 mailboxes ┌────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┐ │ INTERNAL POOL │ CLIENT POOL │ │ 5 domains / 10 mailboxes │ 15 domains / 30 mailboxes │ ├────────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────────────┤ │ growth-getblackink.com (2) │ Client 1: 3 domains / 6 mailboxes │ │ connect-getblackink.com (2) │ Client 2: 3 domains / 6 mailboxes │ │ audit-getblackink.com (2) │ Client 3: 3 domains / 6 mailboxes │ │ pm-getblackink.com (2) │ Client 4: 3 domains / 6 mailboxes │ │ scale-getblackink.com (2) │ Client 5: 3 domains / 6 mailboxes │ │ (client_id = NULL) │ (client_id = that client) │ └────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┘

The unit: one client = one cluster

LevelCountRule
Cluster1 per client= 3 domains, sharing a cluster_label
Domains3 per clustereach has SPF/DKIM/DMARC; +reserve(s) for the sentinel to swap in
Mailboxes6 per cluster2 per domain; the picker rotates across all 6 (LRU)
Cap30–50 / mailbox / dayso one cluster sends ~180–300 cold emails/day

15 client domains ÷ 3 = 5 client clusters at launch (founding clients 1–2 provisioned first). Both the outbound sequence and the inbound Speed-to-Lead cadence send through this same per-client cluster — never the internal pool, never another client's.
